How to Check If Your PC Has a Bottleneck

A PC bottleneck occurs when one component limits the performance of another. The most common bottleneck in gaming is a mismatch between CPU and GPU.

The fastest way to check is using a free PC Bottleneck Calculator that shows your exact bottleneck percentage in 30 seconds.

What Causes a CPU Bottleneck

When your CPU cannot process game logic fast enough to keep the GPU supplied with work, GPU usage drops below 70 percent while CPU cores run at 100 percent. This is a CPU bottleneck.

What Causes a GPU Bottleneck

When your GPU cannot render frames fast enough, it runs at 95 to 100 percent while the CPU has spare capacity. This is normal at 1440p and 4K and is the healthy bottleneck you want.

How to Fix a Bottleneck
